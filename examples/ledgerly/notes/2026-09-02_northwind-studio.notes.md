# 2026-09-02_northwind-studio

## Customer context
Northwind Studio is a design studio. Dana Holt speaks for it and handles invoicing and cash planning. The studio runs projects in three milestones: strategy, identity, guidelines. It has a payroll on the 25th of each month.

## Pains
**Said**
- Ledgerly thinks in months, while the studio bills per project milestone (Dana Holt).
- Dana Holt cannot see in advance what cash arrives before payroll.

**Shown**
- Dana Holt copies the last invoice and edits the milestone line by hand for every milestone.
- Dana Holt guesses the cash forecast by checking which milestones should close and hoping clients sign off on time.

## Gains
- Know next month's incoming cash early in the month, before payroll, and see which clients it comes from (Dana Holt).
- Invoicing that follows project milestones instead of the calendar month.

## Their words
- "the app thinks in months and we think in projects"
- "Knowing next month"
- "I guess"

## Workflow
- Invoice when a milestone closes: three invoices per project, never one at month end.
- To invoice, copy the last invoice in Ledgerly, change the milestone line, send by hand.
- To forecast cash, list the milestones that should close and assume clients sign off on time.

## Tools and alternatives
