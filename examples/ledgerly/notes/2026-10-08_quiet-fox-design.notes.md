# 2026-10-08_quiet-fox

## Customer context
- Three-person design studio.
- Sends about fifteen invoices a month, mostly to tech startups.
- The call is a typed-up record with no recording and no speaker labels.

## Pains
**Said**
- Late payment is the biggest problem. Startups pay when their funding lands, not when the invoice is due.
- Email reminders get ignored, so they chase by phone.
- They add a late fee by hand, then feel bad and remove it.

**Shown**
- Chasing by phone is a workaround for ignored reminders.
- Adding then removing the late fee shows they want a fee that does not need a personal decision each time.

- A late fee that applies by itself after thirty days.
- Getting paid closer to the due date.
- An invoice that makes the studio look professional.
