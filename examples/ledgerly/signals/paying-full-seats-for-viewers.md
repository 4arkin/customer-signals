# Paying full seats for people who only view

> Teams grow, but only a few people send invoices. Paying a full seat for someone who only looks at numbers feels wrong and pushes customers to look elsewhere.

## Evidence

- "Our old tool charged per seat. We are six people, and only two of us ever send an invoice, but we paid for six." — Ravi Shah · Pixel & Pine · 2026-09-09 · call `2026-09-09_pixel-pine`
- "The rest only need to see the numbers. Paying a full seat for someone who reads a dashboard feels wrong." — Jonas Weber · Brightline · 2026-09-22 · call `2026-09-22_brightline`
- "We hired two juniors who only need to see the invoice status. I do not want to pay full seats for them." — Dana Holt · Northwind Studio · 2026-10-20 · call `2026-10-20_northwind-studio`
