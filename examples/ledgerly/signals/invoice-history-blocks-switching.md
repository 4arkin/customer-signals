# Years of invoice history hold them in the old tool

> Past invoices sit in an old tool or sheet, and losing or splitting that history nearly stops the switch. Importing history removes the barrier.

## Evidence

- "Seven years of invoices sit in the old tool. We still pay for it every month only to look things up." — Ravi Shah · Pixel & Pine · 2026-09-09 · call `2026-09-09_pixel-pine`
- "If I can not bring them along, my tax history sits in two places." — Leo Marchetti · Leo Marchetti · 2026-10-01 · call `2026-10-01_leo-marchetti`
- "Five years of invoices were in it, and I did not want to lose them." — Tomas Lind · Tomas Lind · 2026-10-27 · call `2026-10-27_tomas-lind`
