# Invoices when a project phase is approved, not by calendar month

> Studios bill per project milestone: an invoice goes out when the client signs off a phase. A month-based invoicing model does not fit how they work.

## Evidence

- "Three invoices per project, never one at month end." — Dana Holt · Northwind Studio · 2026-09-02 · call `2026-09-02_northwind-studio`
- "the client gets an invoice when they approve each phase." — Ravi Shah · Pixel & Pine · 2026-09-09 · call `2026-09-09_pixel-pine`
- "By phase. The invoice goes out when the client signs off a phase, not on a date." — Jonas Weber · Brightline · 2026-09-22 · call `2026-09-22_brightline`
