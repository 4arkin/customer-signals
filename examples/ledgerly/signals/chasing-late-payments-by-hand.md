# Chasing late payments is manual and awkward

> Getting paid takes more effort than sending the invoice. Customers chase by email, phone, sticky notes or red cells, and the chasing feels awkward with clients they want to keep.

## Evidence

- "Sending them is fine now. Getting paid is the problem. I spend every Friday morning chasing late invoices by email." — Dana Holt · Northwind Studio · 2026-09-02 · call `2026-09-02_northwind-studio`
- "I have a sticky note on my screen with the names of clients to chase. That should not be my system." — Ravi Shah · Pixel & Pine · 2026-09-09 · call `2026-09-09_pixel-pine`
- "When a publisher is late I wait two more weeks before I dare to write, because I do not want to look desperate." — Hana Okafor · Hana Okafor · 2026-09-15 · call `2026-09-15_hana-okafor`
- "nobody sees who is overdue across the agency" — Jonas Weber · Brightline · 2026-09-22 · call `2026-09-22_brightline`
- "I track who owes me in the same sheet, with a red cell." — Leo Marchetti · Leo Marchetti · 2026-10-01 · call `2026-10-01_leo-marchetti`
