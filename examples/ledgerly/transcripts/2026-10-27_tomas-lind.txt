Mira Tan: Tomas, you work with clients in Sweden and Germany. What made you try Ledgerly now?
Tomas Lind: I looked at you last year and stayed with my old tool. Five years of invoices were in it, and I did not want to lose them.
Mira Tan: What changed?
Tomas Lind: My accountant in Stockholm. She asked me to pick a tool that exports to Fortnox, so she stops typing my invoices in.
Mira Tan: How do the German invoices go?
Tomas Lind: They make me nervous. Reverse charge, VAT number, the right sentence on the invoice. I copy the sentence from an old invoice every time.
Mira Tan: What would good look like?
Tomas Lind: I pick the client's country, and the app puts the right VAT text on the invoice.
Mira Tan: Anything else in your week?
Tomas Lind: I track hours in Toggl and copy them into each invoice by hand.
