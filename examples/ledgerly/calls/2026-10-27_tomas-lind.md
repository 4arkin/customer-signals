Mira Tan: Does Ledgerly help there?

Tomas Lind: It has a field for the VAT number, but it does not tell me what to write. I copy the sentence from an old invoice.

Mira Tan: What would good look like?

Tomas Lind: I pick the client's country and the app puts the right VAT text on the invoice. That is it.

Mira Tan: And getting paid?

Tomas Lind: Fine, mostly. Swedish clients pay on time. The Germans pay on the last day, but they pay.
