Mira Tan: You work alone, right? How do you handle invoices today?

Hana Okafor: Alone, yes. Mostly book covers. I send maybe six invoices a month.

Mira Tan: Why did you choose an invoicing app at all?

Hana Okafor: The invoice is the last thing the art director sees from me. If it looks cheap, my cover looks cheap.

Mira Tan: Who else works with your invoices?

Hana Okafor: My accountant. She does my taxes in FreshBooks, and every quarter I send her a zip of PDFs. If she could log in herself, I would not think twice about the price.

Mira Tan: What is the hardest part of the work?

Hana Okafor: Asking for the money. When a publisher is late, I wait two more weeks before I dare to write.

Mira Tan: Anything you miss in the app?

Hana Okafor: Half my clients are in the UK. I never know which VAT text to put on the invoice, and the app does not tell me.
