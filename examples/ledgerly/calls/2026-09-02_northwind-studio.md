Mira Tan: Thanks for making time. How do invoices work at Northwind today?

Dana Holt: We invoice when a milestone closes. Strategy, then identity, then the guidelines. Three invoices per project, never one at month end.

Mira Tan: How does Ledgerly handle that?

Dana Holt: I copy the last invoice, change the milestone line and send it by hand. It works, but the app thinks in months and we think in projects.

Mira Tan: What would make the biggest difference?

Dana Holt: Knowing next month. Payroll is on the twenty-fifth. In the first week of the month I want to see what cash comes in before then, and from whom.

Mira Tan: How do you find that out now?

Dana Holt: I guess. I look at which milestones should close and hope the clients sign them off on time.
