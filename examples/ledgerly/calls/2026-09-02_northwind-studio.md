Mira Tan: Thanks for making time. How are invoices going since you switched?

Dana Holt: Sending them is fine now. Getting paid is the problem. I spend every Friday morning chasing late invoices by email.

Mira Tan: How many are late in a normal month?

Dana Holt: Out of thirty, maybe eight. And each one is an awkward money conversation with a client we want to keep.

Mira Tan: Do you use the reminder feature?

Dana Holt: I turned it off. It sends the same cold text to everyone. A client of five years got the same message as a one-off job.

Mira Tan: What would you want instead?

Dana Holt: Reminders I can write per client, in my voice. Friendly for the regulars, firm for the ones who always pay sixty days late.

Mira Tan: Anything else on your list?

Dana Holt: We bill two clients in dollars and one in Swiss francs. I still do those invoices in a spreadsheet because the VAT line comes out wrong.
